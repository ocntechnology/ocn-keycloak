civicos-password-blocklist.txt: common and breached passwords Keycloak refuses (password policy
passwordBlacklist, compared in lower case, loaded once at start-up; no network calls at sign-in).

Only passwords of 12 to 128 characters are listed, because the policy refuses anything shorter.

Sources (merged, lower-cased, duplicates removed, in this order):
1. The NCSC "100,000 most common passwords" (PwnedPasswordsTop100k), the earlier CivicOS list.
2. "xato-net-10-million-passwords.txt" (Mark Burnett's 10 million passwords, released into the
   public domain), from SecLists, Passwords/Common-Credentials, MIT licence:
   https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists
